CHANGING THE NAME
=================
"The Angul Almanac" is applied. To use a different one, open index.html
in any text editor (Notepad works) and change these four lines. Nothing
else depends on the name.

  1.  <title>The Angul Almanac — Talcher</title>
  2.  <h1>The Angul <em>Almanac</em></h1>
         The <em> wraps the second word; it renders in the accent green.
         For a one-word name:  <h1>Later<em>ite</em></h1>
  3.  "name" and "short_name" inside manifest.webmanifest (the phone app name)
  4.  the <meta name="description"> line, for link previews

DO NOT CHANGE
  The storage name "bl_v7" and the store name "balcony-ledger" inside
  index.html. They are invisible internal labels. Changing either one
  makes the app forget everything you have logged.
