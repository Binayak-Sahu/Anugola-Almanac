CHANGING THE NAME
=================
"The Anugola Almanac" is applied. To use a different one, change these
four places. Nothing else depends on the name.

  1.  index.html — the <title> line
        <title>The Anugola Almanac — Talcher</title>

  2.  index.html — the heading inside the rail
        <h1>The Angul <em>Almanac</em></h1>
      The <em> wraps the second word; it renders in the accent colour.
      For a one-word name:  <h1>Later<em>ite</em></h1>

  3.  manifest.webmanifest — "name" and "short_name"
      (this is what appears under the icon on your phone)

  4.  index.html — the <meta name="description"> line, for link previews

  Optionally: src/main.js sets the browser tab title per screen as
  `${v.label} — The Anugola Almanac`. Change that string too if you want
  it to match.


THE OTHER OPTIONS

  The Dry Gap         March to May is the constraint that decides

  Laterite            One word, the soil you are actually gardening in.
  The Standing Orders Treats it as instructions to yourself.
  Sun & Slab          A container orchard on concrete in Odisha.


DO NOT CHANGE
  · The storage key "almanac_v10" in src/core/schema.js.
    Changing it makes the app forget everything you have logged.

  · The legacy keys "bl_v7" and "bl_v6" in the same file. They are how
    data from the older versions is found and carried across.

  · The IndexedDB name "balcony-ledger" in src/core/persist.js.
    It is a leftover name from the very first version and it looks
    wrong, but every photo you have ever saved is filed under it.
    Renaming it orphans all of them.

  · The cache name prefix "almanac-" in sw.js. Bumping the VERSION
    string there is correct and expected on every deploy; changing the
    prefix means old caches are never cleaned up.
