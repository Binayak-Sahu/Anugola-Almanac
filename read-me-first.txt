IF YOU READ ONLY ONE THING

Two ways to use this. Both work.

  ON YOUR COMPUTER, RIGHT NOW
  Double-click index.html. That is it. Everything works — no internet
  needed, nothing to install, nothing to set up. Your data saves itself
  in that browser.

  ON YOUR PHONE, AS AN APP
  Put it online once (instructions below, about four minutes), then open
  the address on your phone and choose "Add to Home Screen". You get a
  proper app icon. It opens with no signal. It survives being in the yard.

You do not need to understand any of the files. You never need to open
any of them.

WHAT CHANGED IN v9

  ONE CATALOGUE
  Houseplants, fruit trees, vegetables and seed packets are now in the
  same list — 225 entries instead of 121. Search finds all of them. You
  can add ANY of them to the buy list, and press "I own this" on any of
  them to start tracking it. Before, only houseplants could do that.

  New filters across the top: what kind of thing it is, which zone it
  belongs in (A to E, or indoors), and a verdict —

      Works here            plant it, it will fruit
      Works, with a catch    read the note first
      Ruled out — and why    do not buy it, here is the reason

  The "ruled out" list is deliberate. Apple, litchi, peach, mangosteen,
  durian and Alphonso mango are all sold in Indian nurseries and none of
  them will fruit for you. Each entry says exactly why. That list will
  save you more money than the rest of the app.

  48 FRUIT TREES AND VARIETIES
  Not just "mango" — Amrapali, Mallika, Dashehari, Kesar, Arka Neelkiran,
  Banganapalli, each with its own verdict. Same for guava, jamun, sapota,
  citrus. Every entry tells you how it is propagated (this matters more
  than anything else: a grafted mango fruits in 3 years, a seedling takes
  8 and will not taste like the parent), how long to first fruit, and the
  final bag size.

  FEED — A WHOLE NEW SECTION
  Everything about fertiliser in one place:
    · How to read a yellow leaf. The trick is looking at WHICH leaves
      are affected before you look at the colour. Old leaves and new
      leaves mean opposite things.
    · A dose calculator — pick a product and a bag size, it tells you
      the grams and the litres.
    · What the numbers on the bag mean (19:19:19 and so on).
    · Organic inputs and when each one wins.
    · Why pH decides whether any of it works at all. This matters here
      because coal ash makes your soil alkaline.
    · Salt buildup, and how to flush it. Your ledger already shows one
      plant suffering from this.
    · A month-by-month feeding year, including the month you feed
      nothing at all.

  PHONE
    · Five buttons along the bottom instead of fourteen. Everything else
      is behind "More".
    · Text fields no longer make the screen zoom in when you tap them.
      (This was the most annoying bug on the whole site.)
    · Wide tables now stack into readable rows instead of running off
      the side of the screen.
    · Works with no signal once installed.
    · A yellow bar appears at the bottom when you are offline.

  FIXES
    · Watering advice for outdoor plants was wrong. It was reading
      phrases like "Rarely" and turning them into "every 10 days". Each
      outdoor plant now carries a real number for each of the four
      seasons, and shows the written rule when there is no fixed number.
    · Outdoor plants were being labelled "At the glass" and judged
      against a model of your indoor room. They are now labelled by
      their real zone and left out of the room calculations.
    · Keyboard shortcuts now reach every section.
    · You can bookmark a specific plant, not just a section.

PUTTING IT ON YOUR PHONE — about four minutes

  1. Go to  netlify.com/drop  in a browser.
  2. Drag this whole folder onto that page. Wait for it to finish.
     It gives you an address like  something-random.netlify.app
  3. Click "Site configuration", then "Environment variables",
     then "Add a variable".
         Name:   LEDGER_KEY
         Value:  any password you will remember
     This is the only lock on your data. Without it, anyone who guesses
     the address can read and change your ledger.
  4. Click "Deploys", then "Trigger deploy", then "Deploy site".
     (The password only takes effect after this step. Do not skip it.)
  5. Open the address on your phone.
       iPhone:  Share button, then "Add to Home Screen"
       Android: three-dot menu, then "Install app"
  6. In the app, go to Memory, then Sync, type the same password,
     and press Connect. Do this on every device you use.

  Same password everywhere, or your phone and laptop will not see each
  other's changes.


ALREADY HAVE v8 ONLINE?
  Drag the folder onto your existing site instead of making a new one.
  Do not touch the password or the function. Everything you have logged
  carries over — pots, photos, watering history, buy list.


WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
  · In whichever browser you used, always.
  · In your Netlify site, if you connected it.
  · In Memory → "Bake into this file", which downloads a complete backup
    copy of the whole app with your data inside it. Keep one somewhere.
    Do this before any big change.

  Careful: that downloaded copy and the online copy then drift apart. If
  you later drag the OLD downloaded copy back onto Netlify, it replaces
  the newer data. When in doubt, deploy the folder, not a backup.

WHAT TO DO FIRST, THIS WEEK

  Outdoors — this is time-critical. Build and cure the two soil mixes
  now. The coco peat must be soaked in calcium nitrate first or it will
  quietly starve your plants of calcium. The mix then needs five to seven
  days covered in the shade before anything goes into it. Pot up the
  eight plants in early September. Feed nothing until about 1 October.

  Indoors — buy the rack and the grow light before buying another plant.
  Half the catalogue cannot survive that room on window light alone.

  And do the crush-and-smell test on the three tall plants. It takes two
  seconds and it is still marked unresolved.


May in that room models to around 50°C against a 45.3°C outdoor average.
March through June is the hard part, not winter. Plan for shade.
